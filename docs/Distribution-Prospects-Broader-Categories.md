# Relay prospects across sales creative and web work

8 November 2026 · Owner: Shant · All messages unsent

This is the active first outreach batch, broadening the earlier company-research and real-estate comparison. Real estate remains a warm supplier opportunity, not the focus of Relay's positioning. The earlier [research pack](Week-One-Prospects-and-Outreach.md) remains available for additional prospects and interview questions.

Research covers six supplier or technology-partner prospects and six potential buyers across three tracks. Cogniflow, SalesCaptain and SalesRoads are retained from the previous list, not new leads. Public product descriptions establish relevance; no company has agreed to supply, purchase or integrate. Target roles are suggestions, not verified individual contacts.

## What to test

| Track | First outcome to discuss | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales research | A qualified company list with agreed fields, sources and explicit unknowns | Buyer has a recent research job; supplier can substantiate fit and price it |
| Creative production | Five ad variations or three short video cuts from a supplied brand/product brief | Brand fidelity, asset rights, usable exports and bounded revisions |
| Web and data | A scoped landing page OR an extracted, structured dataset | Treat these as separate jobs; establish output ownership, QA and any ongoing costs |

These are proposed offers, not proven demand rankings. Interview buyers before fixing quantity, price or scope. A response from a supplier is not evidence of paying buyer demand.

## Supplier and partner shortlist

### Sales research

**1. Cogniflow — first-wave operator prospect**

- Evidence: describes deployed lead research/enrichment agents and sales workflows. [Official site](https://cogniflow.nl/)
- Ask: can an existing workflow deliver a cited company list through an endpoint under supplier-defined quote rules?
- Contact: founder or solutions lead via public `info@cogniflow.nl` or the site's contact form.
- Unknown: reusable execution API, willingness to take external jobs, permitted data sources and sample quality. Do not commission new development just to qualify a prospect.

**2. Persana — second-wave platform partnership prospect**

- Evidence: markets sales agents and publishes a company/contact enrichment API. [Product](https://persana.ai/), [API](https://persana.ai/full-contact-api)
- Ask: can an operator deliver a complete qualified list, rather than Relay merely purchasing enrichment calls?
- Contact: partnerships/product lead through the [official contact form](https://persana.ai/contact-us).
- Unknown: task-level execution and downstream data rights. The homepage announces joining forces with Rox; confirm the current partnership owner and API availability before discussing implementation. This is not assumed to be an independent small builder.

### Creative production

**3. Creatify — first-wave agent product prospect**

- Evidence: publishes an ad-generation agent, API and an official Ad Agent MCP plugin. [API/product](https://creatify.ai/api), [official plugin repository](https://github.com/creatify-ai/creatify-ad-agent-skill)
- Ask: deliver reviewable video-ad variants from a buyer's brief and permitted assets, without automatically publishing ads.
- Contact: partnerships/API lead through Book Demo on the official product page; describe the inquiry as a distribution partnership.
- Unknown: resale terms, minimum commitments, revision handling, rights and whether they will own fulfillment. Technical access does not prove marketplace interest.

**4. Placid — supporting technology partner, not a qualified agent operator**

- Evidence: template-driven image, PDF and video generation via API, with MCP listed. [Features](https://placid.app/features)
- Ask: introductions to existing operators producing brand-consistent creative packs, or a partnership with an operator who owns the full workflow.
- Contact: partnerships/founder through Contact Us on the official site.
- Unknown: operator availability, template setup costs and distribution terms. Generating a template variant is not the same as doing open-ended creative direction. Do not count Placid as activated supply.

### Web and data work

**5. 10Web — second-wave website platform partner**

- Evidence: a website-builder API explicitly offered for embedding under another brand. [Product](https://10web.io/website-builder-api/), [API documentation](https://apidocs.10web.io/)
- Ask: a scoped website delivered with clear handover, editing and hosting terms; identify who handles revisions.
- Contact: API partnerships through Book a Strategy Call on the product page.
- Unknown: economics for one-off work, export/ownership, recurring charges and operator responsibility. Do not promise a portable source-code ZIP or free hosting from the existence of this API.

**6. Browse AI — first-wave managed data-work prospect**

- Evidence: data extraction, monitoring, scraping services, REST API and webhooks. [Official site](https://www.browse.ai/)
- Ask: an operator-owned extraction job with an agreed schema, coverage, provenance and a usable dataset.
- Contact: managed services/partnerships lead through Contact Us or Schedule a Demo in the site's footer.
- Unknown: whether they accept bounded outcome-based jobs, target-site permissions, maintenance responsibilities and minimum commercial commitments. A scraping platform alone is not a supplier who guarantees completion.

## First week action and comparison

1. Approach Cogniflow, Creatify and Browse AI as three different supply conversations. Keep the existing real estate builder warm separately.
2. Approach SalesCaptain, SalesRoads, Pilothouse and Conversion Factory for concrete buyer interviews. Use CTC and Powered by Search to expand the sample.
3. Ask each buyer for the last comparable job, current process, time/cost, next required date, budget owner and rejection criteria. Request a non-confidential brief only if they are interested.
4. Ask suppliers for an existing sample they have permission to share, one quote and one revision/failure example. Separate product capability from willingness to fulfill jobs through Relay.
5. Compare actual briefs and fulfillment readiness. Do not choose a category based on who likes the marketplace idea most.

This is an initial twelve-company batch, not enough to infer market demand or guarantee a particular number of interviews. Recruit more in the categories that produce concrete jobs. Since this buyer list is agency-heavy, later interview in-house teams and end businesses before generalizing to all customers.

Track each contact as: researched, approached, replied, interviewed, sample reviewed, brief received, quote requested, or declined. All twelve currently remain **researched — unsent**. Record buyer provenance separately from builder referrals.

Product demos and rehearsals stay local/sandbox. No public rollout, paid purchase, account creation, API integration, or outreach has been performed as part of this research.
