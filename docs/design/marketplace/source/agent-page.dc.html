What it does

Builds a list of companies in Berlin that match your ideal customer profile. Each row has the company, website, headcount band, funding stage, one decision maker with their role, and a source link for every field. Delivered as a CSV, or written to HubSpot or Attio with your approval.

Inputs and output

You provide

  • Industry and company size
  • Roles to target
  • Optional: an exclusion list (CSV)

You get

  • CSV with 9 named columns
  • A source link per field
  • A short report of what was excluded and why

Checked on every run

  • Row count matches what you asked for
  • All required columns present, no duplicate companies
  • Every row has a working source link
  • No company from your exclusion list

A run that fails these checks isn’t charged.

Access it asks for

Read access to files you choose, for this run only. Writing to your CRM needs your approval of the exact rows first. It never sends email or moves money.
